# Harborlight Logistics - vendor contract guidelines (synthetic)

Internal notes from the legal team of a fictional company, used as intake
for the playbook-codification eval.

- We buy software; we are always the customer.
- Liability cap: we want 12 months of fees paid or payable. We have taken 6
  months with a USD 250,000 floor. Never below 3 months.
- Anything touching our customer data (breaches, indemnity for data
  misuse) must sit outside the cap or under a super-cap of at least 3x.
- Vendors may never train models on our data. Feedback licences are fine if
  limited to improving the vendor's service.
- Security incidents: 72-hour notice preferred, 5 business days at most.
- No auto-renewal notice windows longer than 30 days. Renewal price rises
  capped at 5% (7% if we must).
- We only indemnify for third-party claims about content we upload. Never
  for "any breach".
- Payment net 45; net 30 acceptable. No mid-term price increases.
- Governing law Ohio preferred; New York or Delaware acceptable.
- Escalate liability and data issues to the Deputy General Counsel.
- Non-solicits: mutual only, 12 months maximum, with a general-advertising
  exception.
