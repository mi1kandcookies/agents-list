MUTUAL NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lumenfield Analytics Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales ("Lumenfield"), and Harborlight Logistics LLC, an Ohio limited liability company ("Harborlight").

1. Definitions
"Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by one party to the other that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.
"Purpose" means evaluating a possible route-analytics partnership between the parties.

2. Obligations
2.1 The receiving party shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and shall protect it with the same care it uses for its own information, but not less than reasonable care.
2.2 Lumenfield may disclose Confidential Information to its affiliates, contractors and investors without restriction.

3. Term
3.1 This Agreement lasts for one (1) year, and the obligations in Section 2 survive for one (1) year after it ends.

4. Remedies
4.1 Harborlight shall indemnify Lumenfield for all losses arising from any unauthorised disclosure by any person.

5. Non-Solicitation
5.1 For three (3) years after this Agreement ends, Harborlight shall not hire or solicit any employee of Lumenfield.

6. Governing Law
6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of London have exclusive jurisdiction.
