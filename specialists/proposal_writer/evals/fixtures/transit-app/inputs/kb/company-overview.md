# Lumen Fieldworks, Inc. - Company Overview

Lumen Fieldworks, Inc. is a fictional software firm founded in 2015 that builds mobile apps for public agencies. The company has 38 employees across two offices.

Lumen Fieldworks has delivered rider information apps for 6 transit agencies. Our real-time arrival engine consumes GTFS-Realtime feeds and refreshes predictions every 15 seconds.

## Accessibility

Every Lumen Fieldworks app release is tested against WCAG 2.1 Level AA using automated scans and moderated sessions with screen reader users on VoiceOver and TalkBack.
