# Security Practices

Lumen Fieldworks encrypts rider data in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256. Location data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Our incident response plan commits to notifying customers of a security incident affecting their data within 48 hours of discovery.

Backend services run on a managed cloud platform with a 99.9% monthly availability target over the last 24 months.
