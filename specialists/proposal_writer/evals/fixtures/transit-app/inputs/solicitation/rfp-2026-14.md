## Section A - Overview

The Harbor Point Regional Transit District (the "District") operates 42 bus routes and a ferry service. The District seeks a vendor to design, build and operate a rider information mobile app for iOS and Android. This solicitation is synthetic sample data for testing.

## Section C - Scope of Work

C.1 Functional Requirements

The app shall display real-time arrival predictions for every bus stop and ferry terminal using the District's GTFS-Realtime feed. The app shall support trip planning across bus and ferry modes. The Contractor shall provide service alerts through push notifications within 60 seconds of publication in the District's alert system.

C.2 Accessibility

The app must conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. The Contractor shall conduct accessibility testing with screen reader users before launch.

C.3 Security and Privacy

The Contractor shall not sell or share rider location data with third parties. The Contractor shall notify the District of any security incident affecting rider data within 72 hours of discovery. Offerors will describe how rider data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

C.4 Hosting and Support

The Contractor shall maintain 99.5% monthly availability for the app's backend services. The Contractor shall provide a support desk during District service hours.

## Section L - Instructions to Offerors

L.1 Proposal Organization

Proposals shall be organized into two volumes: Volume I Technical and Volume II Price. The Technical Volume shall not exceed ten (10) pages. The Price Volume is limited to 3 pages. The Executive Summary shall not exceed 400 words.

L.2 Format

Pages shall be 8.5 x 11 inches with margins of at least 1 inch on all sides. Text shall use a font size of no less than 11 point. Proposals must be submitted as a single PDF file no larger than 25 MB.

L.3 Required Content

Offerors shall describe their technical approach to real-time arrival predictions. Offerors shall provide at least two past performance references for projects of similar scope completed within the last five years. Offerors shall identify key personnel and include a resume for the proposed project manager. The signed Form P-1 Offeror Certification is required with each proposal.

Letters of support are not required.

## Section M - Evaluation

Proposals will be evaluated on the following criteria: technical approach (40 points), past performance (30 points), key personnel (15 points), and price (15 points). The District may award to other than the lowest priced offeror.
