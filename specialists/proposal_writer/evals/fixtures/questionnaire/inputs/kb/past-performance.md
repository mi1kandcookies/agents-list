# Past Performance

## Cedar Valley Transit Authority (fictional)

From 2022 to 2024 Lumen Fieldworks built and operated the Cedar Valley Transit rider app, serving 120,000 monthly active riders. Contract value was $640,000. Reference: operations director, Cedar Valley Transit Authority.

## Northgate Ferry Cooperative (fictional)

In 2023 Lumen Fieldworks delivered a ferry departure app with push service alerts for the Northgate Ferry Cooperative. Alerts reach riders within 30 seconds of publication.
